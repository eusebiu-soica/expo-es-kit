# Official data types, purposes and definitions

Source of truth for the ids used in `sdk-catalog.json`, `privacy-scan.mjs` output and the generated answers. Verified 2026-10-07 against:

- Apple App Privacy Details: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Apple privacy manifest keys (`NSPrivacyCollectedDataType*`): https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/describing-data-use-in-privacy-manifests
- Google Play Data safety: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- Google Play account deletion: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111

Wording in quotes is Apple's or Google's. If a store page changes, update this file first.

### Apple purposes

| Purpose | id | Manifest constant (`NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypePurpose…`) | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third-Party Advertising | `third_party_advertising` | `ThirdPartyAdvertising` | Displaying third-party ads in your app, or sharing data with entities who display third-party ads |
| Developer's Advertising or Marketing | `developer_advertising` | `DeveloperAdvertising` | First-party ads, marketing communications to your users, sharing with entities who display your ads |
| Analytics | `analytics` | `Analytics` | Evaluating user behavior, feature effectiveness, planning features, audience size/characteristics |
| Product Personalization | `product_personalization` | `ProductPersonalization` | Customizing what the user sees (recommendations, suggestions) |
| App Functionality | `app_functionality` | `AppFunctionality` | Authenticate the user, enable features, prevent fraud, security, server up-time, minimize crashes, scalability/performance, customer support |
| Other Purposes | `other_purposes` | `Other` | Any other purposes not listed |

Note: Apple puts **fraud prevention, security and customer support under App Functionality**. Google has separate purposes for those.

### Apple definitions

- **Collect**: "transmitting data off the device in a way that allows you and/or your third-party partners to access it for a period longer than what is necessary to service the transmitted request in real time." An auth token or IP address sent on a request and **not retained** is not collected.
- **Third-party partners**: "analytics tools, advertising networks, third-party SDKs, or other external vendors whose code you've added to your app." Their collection is yours to declare.
- **Linked to the user**: linked to identity "via their account, device, or other details". Data is linked unless, before collection, direct identifiers (user ID, name) are stripped and the data is manipulated to prevent re-linkage, **and** after collection you never try to re-link it or join it with datasets that allow re-linking. "Personal Information"/"Personal Data" under privacy laws is considered linked.
- **Tracking**: "linking data collected from your app about a particular end-user or device, such as a user ID, device ID, or profile, with Third-Party Data for targeted advertising or advertising measurement purposes, or sharing data collected from your app about a particular end-user or device with a data broker." Includes "placing a third-party SDK in your app that combines user data from your app with user data from other developers' apps to target advertising or measure advertising efficiency, **even if you don't use the SDK for these purposes**." Not tracking: linking only on-device; data brokers using data solely for fraud/security; consumer reporting agencies for credit.
- **On-device only**: "Data that is processed only on device is not 'collected'." Anything derived and sent off device is considered separately.
- **IP address**: if stored, declare by use: precise/coarse location, device ID, or diagnostics.
- **Free-form fields**: declare `other_user_content` (text) and `audio_data` (voice). If you *ask* for a specific type (name, email) or offer a specific upload (photos), declare that type.
- **Web views**: data collected via web traffic must be declared, unless the user is navigating the open web.
- **Apple frameworks** (MapKit, CloudKit, App Analytics): declare what *you* collect through them, not what Apple collects.

### Apple optional disclosure (all four must hold)

1. Not used for tracking.
2. Not used for Third-Party Advertising, your Advertising or Marketing, or Other Purposes.
3. Collected only in infrequent cases that are not part of the app's primary functionality, and optional for the user.
4. Provided by the user in the app's interface, clear what is collected, the user's name or account name is prominently displayed in the submission form, and the user affirmatively chooses to provide it each time.

Typical qualifying case: an optional feedback / support form unrelated to the app's main purpose. "Data collected on an ongoing basis after an initial request for permission must be disclosed." Separate all-criteria exemptions exist for regulated financial services and IRB-approved health research.

### Google definitions and exemptions

- **Collect**: "transmitting data from your app off a user's device", including via libraries, SDKs and webviews you control. Your own backend receiving it = collected.
- **Share**: "transferring user data collected from your app to a third party", server-to-server, on-device to another app, or via an SDK.
- **Not collection** (no disclosure): processed only on device; **ephemeral processing** (only in memory, retained "no longer than necessary to service the specific request in real-time"; you still answer "processed ephemerally" when the type is otherwise declared); end-to-end encrypted so unreadable by anyone but sender and recipient.
- **Not sharing** (still collected): transfer to a **service provider** processing "on behalf of the developer and based on the developer's instructions"; legal purposes; **user-initiated action** "where the user reasonably expects the data to be shared, or based on a prominent in-app disclosure and consent"; fully anonymized data.
- **Required vs optional**: optional only if *all* users can use the app without providing it, or can opt in/out, or choose to provide it manually.
- **IP address**: if used to determine location, declare the location type.
- **Encryption in transit**: "Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?" Answer Yes only if every endpoint is HTTPS/TLS (including SDK traffic).
- **Data deletion**: "Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?" (or automatic deletion/anonymization within 90 days).
- **Account deletion** (answer 13327111): apps that enable account creation (in app or via an outside flow the app directs to) must offer **both** an in-app deletion path **and** a web link resource. The link must load without error, reference the app or developer name as on the store listing, and make account deletion prominent. Data retained for security, fraud prevention or regulatory compliance must be explained (e.g. in the privacy policy). The URL is entered in the Data safety form.
- Every app must complete the form and provide a privacy policy, even if it collects nothing.
